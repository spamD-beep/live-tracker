# Privacy and consent

Collect location only for a stated operational purpose, with informed and revocable consent. Minimize precision, frequency, and retention. Give device owners a clear tracking indicator and an accessible stop control. Restrict access by role, review audit logs, and regularly remove stale data.

Document lawful basis, retention periods, breach response, user access/export/deletion procedures, subprocessors, and regional requirements with qualified counsel. Do not repurpose this system for covert surveillance, intimate-partner tracking, employee monitoring without lawful notice, or bypassing platform permissions.
